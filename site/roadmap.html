Roadmap

From the first release to the tree

The firmware and the utility are released. What is left is the hardware: printing the figurines, building the garland, and seeing on a real board what the tests could not show. The roadmap in the repository is the one kept up to date.

Released

1.0.0

Firmware 1.0.0.102 and utility 1.0.0.27: 154 effects, the schedule and clock, the portal over HTTPS with its own root, signed updates with a trial and a way back, the public API, and the utility for Windows. Both build again to the byte from their sources.

Milestones

  • 1. Electrical proof of conceptThe Hi-Link supply under load, the MOSFET gate and the standby draw, and the LED count estimate on the gated rail.
  • 2. 3D models and printsThe enclosure is printed. Next: test prints of the figurines in translucent PETG, for light and fit.
  • 3. Firmware on the boardImplemented and tested on the host. Left to see on the board: the screen, gestures and screensavers with no flicker; the portal from a phone; NTP, the schedule and daylight saving; the clock module; every effect on real figurines; an update, a refused signature and a return to the previous firmware.
  • 4. The 32-figurine garlandThe cable harness, the order of the figurines along it, and the bases and caps put together.
  • 5. On the treeA 24-hour run, the current in every mode, and the garland on the Christmas tree.
