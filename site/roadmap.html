Roadmap

From the first release to the tree

The firmware and the utility 1.4.0 are released, the utility for Windows and Linux, with macOS to follow. The releases before them are seen working on the owner's garland, the utility on Windows and on macOS; 1.4.0 is still to be seen there. What is left is the hardware: printing the figurines, building the full garland, and measuring it. The roadmap in the repository is the one kept up to date.

Released

1.4.0 Firmware and utility

Released 9 November 2026. Firmware and utility 1.4.0, the utility for Windows and Linux: a current limit worked out from your power supply, the Scheduler24 screensaver, an update kept only once it has answered, Tune on your garland's own chain, and a start that waits for what it could not read. A garland updated from 1.1.1 keeps all it saved.

1.3.0 Utility

Released 6 November 2026. The utility alone, 1.3.0.44 for Windows, 1.3.0.45 for Linux, and the first for macOS, 1.3.0.42 for Apple silicon and 1.3.0.43 for Intel: an app with its icon, the token in the keychain, the system's own panels for files. It works with the firmware 1.1.0 and 1.1.1 alike.

1.1.1 Firmware

Released 6 November 2026. The firmware alone, 1.1.1.114: the figurines' new order along the cable and their new colours, and settings a start cannot read read again later, never saved over. A garland updated from 1.1.0 keeps all it saved.

1.2.0 Utility

Released 5 November 2026. The utility alone, 1.2.0.38 for Windows and 1.2.0.39 for Linux, the first for Linux: the token in the Secret Service of GNOME Keyring or KWallet, the desktop's own dialog for files, and one window at a time. The firmware stays 1.1.0.113. The Linux file builds again to the byte on Ubuntu 24.04, or in Docker on Windows.

1.1.0 Firmware and utility

Released 4 November 2026. Firmware 1.1.0.113 and utility 1.1.0.37: playlists with cross-fades, fades on turning on and off and a morning rise, backup and restore, the schedule on a 24-hour timeline, push updates, current in Tune, and a check of the API after an update. A garland updated from 1.0.0 keeps all it saved.

1.0.0 Firmware and utility

Released 3 November 2026. Firmware 1.0.0.102 and utility 1.0.0.27: 154 effects, the schedule and clock, the portal over HTTPS with its own root, signed updates with a trial and a way back, the public API, and the utility for Windows. Both build again to the byte from their sources.

What 1.4.0 brought

Tested on the host, and the portal's pages in a browser against a stand-in for the garland; not yet seen on the owner's garland.

A current limit for your supply

The Device page takes your power supply, 500 to 4400 mA, what one LED draws at full white and dark, and a margin of 5 to 20 % of what the supply gives beyond the board, and works out the limit, at most about 3.9 A, and how many LEDs show full white undimmed. One LED measured 36 mA where the firmware counted 55, so the full garland on white is no longer dimmed for nothing. The defaults keep the 1400 mA limit.

Scheduler24

A screensaver for a garland with the clock module that says what comes next and how soon, the next scheduled action within a day or the playlist's next effect, or what happened last and how long ago.

Tune on your chain

The preview and Tune in the utility draw as many LEDs as your garland has, and chart their current, the part past the limit in red.

An update that has to answer

A new firmware keeps itself only once it has answered a paired browser or program, as the portal and the utility ask while they wait, within 10 minutes, and goes back to the one before otherwise. A refusal does not count. Then the portal and the utility show the Info page.

Its own names only

Plain HTTP and what asks for no pairing answer only to the garland's .local name, its address and your domain, so a page whose name DNS turns to the garland gets nothing from it.
