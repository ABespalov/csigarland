Roadmap

From the first release to the tree

The firmware 1.1.1 and the utility 1.3.0, for Windows, Linux and macOS, are released. The firmware 1.1.0 and the utility on Windows and on macOS are seen working on the owner's garland; the firmware 1.1.1 is still to be. What is left is the hardware: printing the figurines, building the full garland, and measuring it. The roadmap in the repository is the one kept up to date.

Released

1.2.0

Released 5 November 2026. The utility alone, 1.2.0.38 for Windows and 1.2.0.39 for Linux, the first for Linux: the token in the Secret Service of GNOME Keyring or KWallet, the desktop's own dialog for files, and one window at a time. The firmware stays 1.1.0.113. The Linux file builds again to the byte on Ubuntu 24.04, or in Docker on Windows.

1.1.0

Released 4 November 2026. Firmware 1.1.0.113 and utility 1.1.0.37: playlists with cross-fades, fades on turning on and off and a morning rise, backup and restore, the schedule on a 24-hour timeline, push updates, current in Tune, and a check of the API after an update. A garland updated from 1.0.0 keeps all it saved.

1.0.0

Released 3 November 2026. Firmware 1.0.0.102 and utility 1.0.0.27: 154 effects, the schedule and clock, the portal over HTTPS with its own root, signed updates with a trial and a way back, the public API, and the utility for Windows. Both build again to the byte from their sources.

Milestones

  • 1. Electrical proof of conceptThe Hi-Link supply under load, the MOSFET gate and the standby draw, and the LED count estimate on the gated rail.
  • 2. 3D models and printsThe enclosure is printed. Next: test prints of the figurines in translucent PETG, for light and fit.
  • 3. Firmware on the boardDone: implemented, tested on the host, and seen on the owner's garland: the screen, gestures and screensavers with no flicker; the portal from a phone; NTP and the schedule; the clock module; every effect on eight figurines, one of each kind; an update, a refused signature and a return to the previous firmware.
  • 4. The 32-figurine garlandThe cable harness, the order of the figurines along it, and the bases and caps put together.
  • 5. On the treeA 24-hour run, the current in every mode, and the garland on the Christmas tree.

What 1.1.0 brought

Tested, and seen working on the owner's garland before its release.

Playlists and transitions

Up to 16 effects played in turn, with a cross-fade from one to the next.

Gentle on and off

Fades on turning on and off, and a scheduled morning turn on that rises from dark over a minute.

A check after an update

The utility reads every route of the public API and holds each answer to its types.

Backup and restore

The schedule, favourites, playlist, screen and your own effects to a file and back, for a sealed unit that may have to be erased.

The schedule on a timeline

The next 24 hours, and where actions meet, on the portal and in the utility.

Push updates

The portal and the utility follow the garland as it changes, rather than asking every few seconds.

Current in Tune

What an effect draws, by the firmware's own model, and where the 1400 mA limit holds it back.

Planned

More platforms

The utility on Android and iOS, on the same Go code and the same public API.
